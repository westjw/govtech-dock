Proposals an agent produced, waiting to go through a door.

These ARE committed, unlike `data/site_pages/` (other people's page text, ~100MB
at full scale) and `data/briefs/` (assembled from it). A proposal is the output
meant to be read: a cloud routine writes them here and opens a pull request, and
`agents.py ingest --kind <k> --file <f>` runs the door over them locally.

Nothing here has been through a door. Nothing here is on the map.
