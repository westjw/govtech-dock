Which company

It has to be an address at the company's own domain. That is the whole proof — we do not ask for a password, and we never store one.

Your page

The one-line description

What you sell and to whom, in one line. This is what shows on the board next to your name.

The longer description

Two or three paragraphs, one per box. Written the way you would explain the company to somebody who has not heard of it — not a brochure. We keep the version we wrote from your site until this is approved.

Post a role

Link it on your own site or your applicant tracking system. We cannot show a job we cannot check is yours. Posted roles expire after 60 days unless your board still carries them.

Where you are filed

This one is a request, not a change. A shelf is only worth something because somebody browsing it expected to find what is on it, so a person decides — we will not move a company because it asked.

Something else

Anything we have wrong that does not fit above. This reaches a person, not a form handler.

Your competitors are not editable. Who a buyer would shortlist against you is the part of this board that has to be independent of you. If one is wrong, say so under “Something else” and a person will read it.
